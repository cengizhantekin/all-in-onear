The All in oNEar thesis

Our long-term thesis starts with what builders can do when a blockchain, cross-chain financial infrastructure, privacy tools and AI services are available in one ecosystem. That combination matters if it helps teams build applications people return to, and if their activity creates durable demand for NEAR.

In Labs, you can inspect the ideas behind the thesis and test specific claims. Follow a payment rehearsal, compare account storage across two deployment methods, or look at how Intents activity translates into fees. Each experiment shows the data used, what the result supports and which questions remain open.

Hubble guides you from the thesis to the experiment and its sources.
